Spotifast 0.16 brings Windows Media Player skins, alongside the Winamp ones. Artist pages gain monthly listeners and upcoming releases, Settings shows what your storage holds, and other devices' changes appear as Spotify pushes them. Soloist is also sturdier: it reconnects after a dropped connection, stops when you quit, and a leftover copy no longer blocks the next start.

## New

- **Windows Media Player skins.** Spotifast can now wear a Windows Media Player skin as well as a Winamp one. By @davorb. ([#1030](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1030))
- **See an artist's monthly listeners** on their page. By @Espaye. ([#1148](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1148))
- **Upcoming releases on artist pages,** with a countdown to the day they arrive. By @imafreakandyalldontknowit67. ([#1129](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1129))
- **Sort an album's songs by plays.** By @DoWhileGeek. ([#1111](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1111))
- **See your storage sizes and clear cached audio** in Settings. By @dyd4dsh7. ([#1113](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1113))
- **Copy a song's details** from a new submenu in the song menu. By @spinfal. ([#1133](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1133))
- **Links in a playlist's description are clickable.** By @Espaye. ([#1150](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1150))
- **Other devices' changes appear as Spotify pushes them,** without waiting for the next check. By @Espaye. ([#902](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/902))
- **Clearer top bar for screen readers and keyboards,** with focus rings on its controls. By @EnricoNSilva. ([#1114](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1114))
