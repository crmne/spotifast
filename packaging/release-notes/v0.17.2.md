## Fixed

- **Soloist's music no longer breaks up while the machine is busy on Linux.** Its audio threads now run realtime through rtkit, ahead of a browser's audio and below PipeWire's own, under a CPU limit that stops a runaway thread instead of letting it stall the desktop. A thread rtkit turns down, as it can for a moment while a browser starts, is asked again until it is raised, rather than left at normal priority until Spotifast restarts. Without rtkit they keep the raised priority they had. By @crmne.
