Spotifast 0.17.2 gets Soloist playing again on Windows, where Spotify's newest build of it crashes on start, and fixes songs that wouldn't start: a song picked on your phone, a song after autoplay took over, and a song far down the queue. Updates now install themselves at the next start, and the heart opens a checklist of your playlists. If you missed 0.17, it brought Windows Media Player skins that really run, two skins of our own and MilkDrop inside them: see the [0.17.0 notes](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.17.0) and the film.

## New

- **Updates install themselves.** A copy that updates itself downloads the new version and installs it the next time Spotifast starts, or right away if you restart. Updates have their own section in Settings, with an **Update automatically** switch. A copy installed by a package manager shows the command that updates it, ready to copy. By @crmne.
- **The heart opens a checklist of your playlists,** with their covers, as on Spotify's phone app: tick or untick Liked Songs and any playlist in one place. By @Espaye in [#1151](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1151).
- **Omarchy themes bring their own shades.** Panels, selections and muted text take the colours the theme defines, instead of blends of its background and foreground. By @diverseau in [#1308](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1308).

## Fixed

- **Soloist plays again on Windows.** Spotify's newest Soloist build crashes as it starts on Windows, so Spotifast said "Soloist didn't start". A new build that keeps crashing now sends Spotifast back to the last build that worked, until Spotify publishes a better one. Reported in [#1220](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/issues/1220). By @crmne.
- **A song picked on your phone plays, not the playlist's first.** The Spotify app names the song in a way librespot misread. The fix is librespot's, by @briandet in [librespot#1795](https://github.com/librespot-org/librespot/pull/1795). Reported in [#1303](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/issues/1303).
- **Songs start again after autoplay took over.** With shuffle on, Soloist and other devices refused every new song with "Restriction violated" once autoplay's radio was playing, even after a restart. By @v14 in [#1348](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1348). Reported in [#1307](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/issues/1307) and [#1346](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/issues/1346).
- **Clicking a song far down the queue plays just that song,** instead of briefly playing every song above it first. By @bompus in [#1317](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1317), who also reported it in [#1316](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/issues/1316).
- **Soloist ends with Spotifast, however Spotifast ends.** Ending it from Task Manager, a crash or a forced quit no longer leaves the player running and listed in Spotify. @n-crespo proposed the same Windows fix in [#1301](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1301). By @crmne.
- **Soloist's music no longer breaks up while the machine is busy on Linux.** Its audio threads now run realtime through rtkit, under a CPU limit, and a thread rtkit turns down for a moment is asked again until it is raised. By @crmne.
- **No more rectangular shadow round skins on Windows.** By @crmne.
- **A Sonos started from your phone can be controlled, and its volume holds,** although Spotify reports it without an id. By @Espaye in [#1193](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1193) and [#1188](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1188).
- **Next up no longer lists a song ten times** when Spotify pads a short queue, and **Recent shows each play once.** By @Espaye in [#1192](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1192) and [#1189](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1189).
- **The sign-in screen shows the whole redirect address again.** By @crmne.
