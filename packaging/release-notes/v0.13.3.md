Spotifast 0.13.3 strengthens sign-in, cover art downloads, MilkDrop preset loading and playback connections. It keeps tokens and credentials out of debug logs and makes playback's push connection use your configured proxy.

On Windows, pick the `.msi`. The setup program (`-setup.exe`) is still attached so that installs from 0.12.0 and earlier can update through it.

## Fixed

- **Sign-in no longer gets held up by another program on your computer.** The local sign-in listener now limits how long it waits for each connection and how much it reads. By @crmne.
- **MilkDrop preset packs stay inside the presets folder.** Pack entries must name a single `.milk` file, including on Windows. By @crmne.
- **Oversized cover art is refused during download.** Downloads are limited as they arrive, and stored zip entries must match their declared size. By @crmne.
- **MilkDrop rejects presets with overly long shader identifiers.** Spotifast carries a projectM fix that prevents overly long identifiers from corrupting memory. By @crmne; thanks @kblaschke. ([projectM #1049](https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/projectm/pull/1049))
- **Tokens and credentials stay out of debug logs, even at trace level.** Spotifast carries the fix in librespot until it is released upstream. By @crmne. ([librespot #1806](https://github.com/librespot-org/librespot/pull/1806))
- **Playback's push connection uses your configured proxy.** Spotifast carries the librespot proxy fix by @photovoltex. ([librespot #1665](https://github.com/librespot-org/librespot/pull/1665))
- **Malformed network handshakes no longer crash playback.** Spotifast carries a librespot fix that rejects oversized handshake packets. By @crmne. ([librespot #1807](https://github.com/librespot-org/librespot/pull/1807))
