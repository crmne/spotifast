On Windows, pick the `.msi`. The setup program (`-setup.exe`) is still attached so that installs from 0.12.0 and earlier can update through it.

## Fixed

- **Music plays without dropouts on Linux.** 0.14.0 kept too little sound ready for PipeWire, so playback stuttered. Spotifast now keeps 200 ms of sound queued, and the visualizers stay in time with it. By @crmne; thanks @jqewdfr13, @ez-ruh and @gcsvieira. (#994)
